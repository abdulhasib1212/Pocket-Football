Controls & Gameplay Guide

WASD / Arrows
Move active player. In Local 2P Versus, Player 1 uses WASD and Player 2 uses Arrow Keys.
X / K
Ground Pass to the highlighted teammate with automatic receiver switch (X for P1, K for P2).
Space / J
Tap to Shoot fast or hold to charge maximum power; press on defense for a Tackle (Space for P1, J for P2).
Q / L
Switch active player between defender and striker (Q for P1, L for P2).
C / Esc
Toggle Dynamic or Full-Pitch camera (C), or pause match anytime (Esc / P).

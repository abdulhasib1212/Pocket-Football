🎮 Controls & Gameplay Guide

WASD / Arrows
Move active player. Ball sticks close to feet with EAFC-style dribbling control!
X / K
Ground Pass. Crisp pass targeting open teammate with instant auto-switch!
Space / J
Shoot on offense (driven shot under crossbar) / Standing Tackle on defense to steal ball!
Q
Manual Switch to other outfield teammate (DF or FW).
C / Wheel
Toggle camera (Dynamic Follow vs Full Pitch) and adjust zoom.
